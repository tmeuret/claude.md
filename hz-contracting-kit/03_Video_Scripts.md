## V2 — Get Prepared to Contract (P4, Tim, ~90 sec)

Here's what carriers are going to ask for, and why.

**Your driver's license.** Carriers verify you are who you say you are.

**Your resident Life and Health license and your NPN.** No carrier can appoint you without them. If you're still studying, this is where you'll come back.

**E&O.** Errors and omissions insurance protects you and your clients. Every carrier requires it. If all your contracts are with me, I cover it.

**AML.** Anti-money-laundering training. Carriers require it before you write annuities and most life products. Do it inside WebCE while you're there.

**A voided check or a bank letter.** That's how you get paid. This one can wait until your first commission, but open a separate business account now. The IRS doesn't like mixing business money with personal money.

**A release from your current upline,** only if you're contracted somewhere else today.

None of these should stop you from finishing this form. Mark what you have, mark what you're working on, and we'll follow up.

## V3 — Banking (P9, Andre, ~75 sec)

Hi, I'm Andre Meuret, CFO at Loreen & Lane.

A couple of things that save you headaches later. First, decide whether you're getting paid as an individual or through your business entity. If it's your entity, the bank account has to be in the entity's name and match your TIN.

Second, keep it separate. A dedicated account makes taxes cleaner and keeps commission money from getting lost in your grocery budget.

Upload a voided check or a letter from your bank. If you're still opening the account, pick "I'm still setting up my account" and keep going. We'll remind you before your first commission.

Once it's approved, open the certificate again and download it, or use Print, then Save as PDF. Upload that PDF here. If you've already submitted the form, use the link in your email to come back and add it.

Remember, this coverage is for agents whose contracts are all with Loreen & Lane.
